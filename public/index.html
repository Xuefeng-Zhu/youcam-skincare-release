Any immediate warning signs?

Trouble breathing or swallowing, or new or sudden swelling around your eyes or of your lips, mouth, tongue, or throat?

For an emergency, use your local emergency number now.

Any concerning rash signs?

A rash covering most of your body, blisters or open/raw skin, rapid spread, pain, fever or feeling ill with a rash, or a rash involving eyes, lips, mouth, or genital skin?

Is it persisting or reacting to products?

Dryness continuing despite basic gentle care, or products causing burning, stinging, or irritation?

Not a diagnosis or a complete warning-sign check. You can leave an answer unknown.
